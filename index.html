Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026
Englishภาษาไทย

Spin Wheel (“the app”) is developed by Cat Tech (“we”, “us”).

In short: Spin Wheel works offline. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The only outside service it uses is Google Play, for the optional Pro purchase.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of our own. Nothing you do in it is sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following only on your device, so your work is still there next time:

  • Your wheels and lists, and the results of the current round
  • Settings: language, theme, colors, and how each mode looks and plays
  • Photos you add to entries (see below)
  • Whether Spin Wheel Pro is unlocked

We cannot see this data. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Purchases

Spin Wheel offers one optional in-app purchase, Spin Wheel Pro, a one-time unlock. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details. To restore a purchase on a new phone, the app asks Google Play whether your account owns Pro; that is handled by Google Play, not by us.

Photos

You can put a photo on an entry by choosing one from your gallery or by taking one with your camera. This only happens when you ask for it, through the system’s photo picker or camera app. The photo is cropped and copied into the app’s private storage. It is never uploaded or shared with us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you remove it from the entry or delete the wheel.

Device backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app’s data in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google under your account, not by us.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. Choosing a photo and taking a picture go through the system’s own photo picker and camera app, and the Pro purchase goes through Google Play.

Children’s privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Third-party services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Distribution and the in-app purchase are provided by Google Play, subject to Google’s own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email vegeta7044@gmail.com.
